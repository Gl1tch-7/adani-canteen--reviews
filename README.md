Campus Canteen Smart Ordering System

A web-based solution designed to streamline food ordering in college canteens, reduce peak-hour overcrowding, and prevent order mix-ups.

🛑 Problem Statement

The traditional canteen workflow requires students to order and pay at one counter, receive a physical token, and hand it over at another counter to pick up their food. During short breaks, this leads to:

Severe Crowding: Long queues and chaos around pickup counters.

Order Mix-ups: Tokens or food items getting misplaced or swapped.

Wasted Time: High wait times for both preparation and pickup.

💡 Solution

This platform digitizes the entire ordering and fulfillment workflow:

Online Ordering: Students place their food orders directly through the website.

Flexible Payments: Choose between online payment or paying cash/UPI at the counter upon pickup.

Real-time Kitchen Dashboard: Canteen staff receive incoming orders instantly on a dashboard to begin preparation.

Order Tracking & Notifications: Students get live updates on their order status (Placed ➔ Preparing ➔ Ready) and receive a notification as soon as their meal is ready for collection.

✨ Key Features

Student Portal:

Interactive food menu with custom customization/item options.

Payment flexibility (Online / Pay at Counter).

Real-time status tracker (Preparing, Ready, Completed).

Automated order-ready notifications.

Canteen Staff Dashboard:

Real-time order queue management.

One-click status updates to notify students.
